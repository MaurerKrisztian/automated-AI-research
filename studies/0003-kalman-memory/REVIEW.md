## What holds up
- **The reference is independent and genuinely extended precision.** `kalman_reference_np` shares no code with the chunk implementation and runs in long double from the float64 inputs. The one shared input, `stationary_noise`, is checked separately by the one-pair formulas and the closed-form φ.
- **No shared bug behind the four-form agreement.** A brute-force construction of 𝒦, X, Z and R from direct gate products and the state-space covariances agrees to ≤ 6.1e-16 over 12 random chunks, for both Gram paths. Per-token relative errors stay ≤ 1.7e-15.
- **Reproducibility.** `check_chunk.py` and `check_float32.py` reproduce their logs (float32 apart from timing lines), and parts A–D are identical across the float64 log versions.
- **Every number** in the Summary, Claims 1–5 and 7–10 and the tables matched the logs and JSONs, correctly rounded, in the version reviewed.
- **The gradient checks are meaningful.** They compare autograd through two independent implementations on a loss over O, M_T and P_T. The central-difference gap scales as h², so it is truncation.
- **The float32 comparison is fair.** Every method gets the same float32 inputs, measured against a float64 reference. "Running only the C×C stage in float64 does not close the gap" holds.
- **Claim 10's mechanism is confirmed.** The log cumulative decay reaches −111.5, A underflows to 0, and the matrix-product 𝒦 fills with infinities and NaNs before the Cholesky fails.
- **Evidence levels and wording are calibrated.** E2 for CPU numerics, E1 for the FLOP model, nothing on a GPU.
- **Novelty.** The reviewer's searches found nothing closer than the Bayesian Layer and Kalman Linear Attention.

#### What holds up
- **Propositions 2–3**, including the general φ_m recursion and the stationary telescoping. Checked by hand, and in 24 float64 cases against the token-by-token filter: scalar and per-channel gates down to 0.02; stationary, free isotropic and free diagonal noise; C from 1 to 33 and T from 1 to 70, including T not a multiple of C; d from 3 to 40; β from 1e-3 to 50. Worst errors: output 6.1e-15, M 1.1e-14, P 6.2e-15.
- **The Cholesky-prefix argument.** The leading block of L⁻¹ is L_{[t]}⁻¹, and the observation covariance does not depend on the reading token. The filter's innovation variances equal diag(L)².
- **Proposition 1.** (a) holds by hand. (c) matches Mesa's readout to 1.2e-15. (d)'s one-pair formulas hold to ≤ 2.8e-16.
- **Proposition 4(b).** No bound was violated in 300 cases with up to 200 tokens of history. The AR(1) row-sum bound is fairly tight: 16.8 against 19 at γ = 0.9, C = 64.
- **Proposition 5 and the Remark** hold to 4.4e-16 and 1.4e-14, and the KDA convention is right.
- **Proposition 6** apart from issue 1. The extra backward cost, 4C³ + 4C²e, is right.
- **Every range in the Summary, Claims and Results** matches `logs/flop_model_v3.log`, and the v1 and v2 logs hold the same tables.
- **The FLOP formulas** match the Cost text term by term, and `cg` and `carried_inv` are identical to study 0002's. The BL count matches BL v5's Appendix C.2–C.3 and is a lower bound for it.
- **The counting is conservative for the new method.** (𝒜Q)P_0 is redundant; dropping it would give 1.05–3.24× BL v5 instead of 1.23–3.38×. Masked products are counted dense, and CG's in-chunk term at its causal average.
- **Citations of studies 0001 and 0002** match their Claims tables.
- **Statements about the cited papers** were checked against Bayesian Layer v5, Kalman Delta Networks, Voltic, Gated KalmaNet, Kalman Linear Attention and Slock & Maouche.
- **"No prior publication found"** for the specific combination is supported. The reviewer's own searches found no exact, chunk-parallel, dense-covariance Kalman memory with per-token readouts from one Cholesky.
- **Wording.** No "novel", "breakthrough" or "state of the art"; E1 is stated throughout. The scratch prototypes are described as non-evidence, and the Limitations are specific.
