## Key results

- **0001 · Prefix-Cholesky: exact chunkwise least-squares memory** (review, E3): One small Cholesky per chunk gives every token its exact least-squares memory readout; with a carried inverse and closed-form backward, a training pass took 21.5 ms vs 68.7 ms for the first version and 621.4 ms for 30 CG steps differentiated by autograd (RTX 5060 Ti, float32).
- **0002 · Exact chunkwise MesaNet: forget gates as a per-token ridge** (review, E2): Forget gates act as a per-token ridge, so one eigendecomposition per chunk plus small Woodbury solves gives exact readouts and closed-form gradients (float64 error ≤ 4.5e-14 when well-conditioned); on an RTX 5060 Ti, with a batched Triton Jacobi eigh, the forward solve is 2.1–8.8× slower than 30-step CG but faster than CG at matched error in 9 of 12 ill-conditioned cells.
- **0003 · Chunk-parallel Kalman memory: one Cholesky per chunk** (review, E2): Verified in float64 (E2): for the Bayesian Layer's Kalman memory, one C×C Cholesky per chunk gives every exact readout (≤ 1.2e-15 over 26 cases) and gradient, leaving only C×C work order-dependent; classical pieces in a new setting; less accurate than the token-by-token filter when cond(𝒦) is large.
