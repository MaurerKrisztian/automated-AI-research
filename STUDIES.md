## Key results

- **0001 · Prefix-Cholesky: exact chunkwise least-squares memory** (review, E3): One small Cholesky per chunk gives every token its exact least-squares memory readout; with a carried inverse and closed-form backward, a training pass took 21.5 ms vs 68.7 ms for the first version and 621.4 ms for 30 CG steps differentiated by autograd (RTX 5060 Ti, float32).
- **0002 · Exact chunkwise MesaNet: forget gates as a per-token ridge** (review, E2): MesaNet's forget gates act as a per-token ridge, so one eigendecomposition per chunk plus small Woodbury solves gives exact readouts without CG (float64 error ≤ 4.5e-14 when well-conditioned) and closed-form gradients; FLOPs ≈ 4–19 CG steps forward, 8–16 vs fla's 60 per training pass; GPU untested.
